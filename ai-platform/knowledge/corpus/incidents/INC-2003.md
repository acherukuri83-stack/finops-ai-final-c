Raised after a USD wire was held with reason `SCREENING` and the client's latest screening
returned `HIT`. Per the Sanctions Procedure, a hit freezes the wire and the only action is
a compliance referral; no routing, reschedule, or standing-instruction change is proposed
while the freeze is in place.

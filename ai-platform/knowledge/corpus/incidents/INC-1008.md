---
kind: incident
incident_id: INC-1008
summary: An outbound wire was held past its Fedwire cutoff for a new-beneficiary control and rolled to the next value date.
root_cause: The beneficiary was not on the client's standing wire instructions, which routes the wire to a reviewer. The review was not completed before the 17:00 ET cutoff, so the wire rolled to the next business day.
resolution: The reviewer released the wire the following morning and the standing instruction was added so future wires to the beneficiary would not hold. This is a wire-operations incident, unrelated to securities settlement.
occurred: 2026-03
---

Recorded for completeness. Wire holds and cutoffs are a separate control surface from
trade settlement and are handled by the wire desk.

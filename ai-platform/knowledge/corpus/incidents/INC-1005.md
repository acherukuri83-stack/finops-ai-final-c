---
kind: incident
incident_id: INC-1005
summary: Settlement failed on a counterparty SSI mismatch; a custodian move had not been reflected in the client's standing settlement instruction.
root_cause: The custodian moved the client's account to a new DTC participant and issued a notice. The client's SSI was never updated to the new participant, so our instruction still named the old one while the counterparty affirmed the new one.
resolution: Retrieved the custodian notice for the account and effective date. Updated the client SSI to the participant in the notice under SSI Policy §3.2, then resubmitted. Re-affirmation was not required because the counterparty was already on the new participant.
occurred: 2026-06
---

The distinguishing evidence was the custodian notice: it showed our own record, not the
counterparty's, was the one out of date. Without the notice the same symptoms would point
to a stale counterparty instruction instead.

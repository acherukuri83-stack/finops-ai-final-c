---
kind: incident
incident_id: INC-1004
summary: Settlement failed on a counterparty SSI mismatch even though both sides named the same participant; the counterparty's instruction on file for us had expired.
root_cause: The delivery instruction the counterparty held on file for us had a valid_to date earlier than the settlement date. The affirmation gateway rejected it as expired even though the participant value matched our current SSI.
resolution: Confirmed our SSI was current and the participant values agreed. Requested a refreshed counterparty instruction for the current SSI and resubmitted. No client SSI change.
occurred: 2026-07
---

The affirmation gateway logged `instruction valid_to <date> < settle_date` for the trade.
This is not a client-side fault; the fix is a refreshed counterparty instruction, not an
`update_ssi`.

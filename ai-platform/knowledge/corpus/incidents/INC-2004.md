Raised when a large USD wire stayed held with reason `FUNDING`. The available balance
returned for the account and currency was below the wire amount. Per Wire Processing Guide
§7.4 the wire remains held and the funding shortfall is raised; the wire itself carries no
proposed action until funding is confirmed.

incident_id: INC-2002
summary: A EUR wire missed the TARGET2 same-day cutoff and an operator attempted to route it for same-day release.
root_cause: The wire was worked after 15:00 and a route-to-reviewer action was proposed for a same-day value date that was no longer attainable.
resolution: Withdrew the routing action and proposed reschedule_value_date to the next business day. Confirmed the value date moved and the wire remained held for the reviewer at the new date.
occurred: 2026-06
---

Raised when a EUR wire to a known beneficiary was still held at 15:38 with hold reason
`AWAITING_RELEASE`. The cutoff monitor had logged the 15:00 TARGET2 cutoff as passed. The
correct action after the cutoff is `reschedule_value_date`, not a same-day route. No forced
release was made.

summary: A duplicate booking failed to settle; two identical trades were entered seconds apart, one settled and the other was flagged as a duplicate suspect.
root_cause: A booking was submitted twice from an upstream system within a few seconds. Both trades had the same account, security, side, quantity, and price. The settlement engine settled the first and failed the second with DUPLICATE_SUSPECT.
resolution: Confirmed the pair with a trade search and the booking-service log. Proposed cancel_trade on the later id with both trade ids in the impact, and confirmed the cancellation with the trader before it was approved. The failed trade was not resubmitted.
occurred: 2026-04
---

The booking-service log showed two INFO lines for the pair a few seconds apart, and the
settlement engine log referenced the first id as the duplicate-of on the failed trade.

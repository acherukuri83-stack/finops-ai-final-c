---
kind: incident
incident_id: INC-2001
summary: An outgoing wire was held for a new beneficiary and released late after the review packet omitted the same-day cutoff.
root_cause: The review packet routed to the reviewer did not carry the currency cutoff, so the reviewer acted after the Fedwire same-day window had closed.
resolution: Re-issued the packet with the cutoff time and minutes remaining. Added the cutoff line to the standard new-beneficiary review packet. The wire settled the next business day on a rescheduled value date.
occurred: 2026-05
---

Raised after a USD wire to a first-time beneficiary sat in the reviewer queue past the
16:00 cutoff. The hold reason was `NEW_BENEFICIARY`; the beneficiary account was not on the
client's standing instructions. The packet contained the wire detail and the standing-
instruction check but no cutoff. Operations proposed adding the beneficiary to standing
instructions as a separate action; that action was approved independently of the routing.

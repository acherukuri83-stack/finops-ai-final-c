---
kind: incident
incident_id: INC-1002
summary: Settlement failed on a security identifier mismatch; the CUSIP on the trade did not resolve to the ISIN held in the security master.
root_cause: The security master carried a corrected CUSIP for the instrument; trades booked from a stale downstream cache still carried the old CUSIP, which the settlement validation rejected.
resolution: Escalated to the reference data team, who confirmed the master value. The affected trades were held, not resubmitted, until the downstream cache was refreshed and the bookings corrected.
occurred: 2026-05
---

The reference-data service logged the offending CUSIP for each affected trade. Operations
could not amend the master and did not attempt a resubmission, since validation runs
against the same record.

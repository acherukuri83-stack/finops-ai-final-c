---
kind: incident
incident_id: INC-1001
summary: Settlement failed on a counterparty SSI mismatch; the counterparty affirmed against a superseded DTC participant after the client's SSI was updated.
root_cause: The counterparty had not adopted the client's updated standing settlement instruction. Their affirmation referenced the prior DTC participant while the client's current SSI named the new one.
resolution: Confirmed from SSI history that the client instruction was current. Requested re-affirmation from the counterparty against the current SSI; the trade settled on resubmission. No client SSI change was made.
occurred: 2026-06
---

Raised after a single equity trade failed `COUNTERPARTY_SSI_MISMATCH` the morning after
an SSI change took effect. The settlement engine log showed our instruction and the
counterparty affirmation naming different participants. SSI history showed one current
version covering the settlement date; the counterparty's affirmation timestamp predated
the awareness of the change on their side.

incident_id: INC-2005
summary: A wire named a beneficiary account that conflicted with the client's standing instruction for that beneficiary and was whitelisted instead of reviewed.
root_cause: The operator added the differing account to the client's standing instructions rather than routing the wire to a reviewer, so a mismatch was resolved without a maker-checker decision.
resolution: Reverted the standing-instruction change. Routed the wire to a WIRE_REVIEWER with a packet noting the conflict against the existing instruction. Confirmed the review before release.
occurred: 2026-03
---

Raised after a USD wire to a named beneficiary used an account that did not match the
account on the client's standing instruction for that same beneficiary. A mismatch is a
maker-checker decision: the wire is routed for review, not whitelisted by adding the new
account to standing instructions.
